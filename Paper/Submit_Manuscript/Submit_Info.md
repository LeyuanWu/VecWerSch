### Three-sentence novelty statement
This paper introduces the first open-source Python package dedicated to polyhedral gravity forward modelling, offering a robust, high-performance implementation of the full gravity tensor (potential, vector, and gradient) for arbitrarily complex 3D bodies. By leveraging Numba just-in-time compilation, it achieves computational efficiency comparable to compiled-language codes while maintaining Python’s accessibility and ease of integration. The package provides two algorithmic modes—memory-optimized and speed-optimized—making it uniquely suited for both small-scale geophysical studies and large planetary applications, filling a critical gap in open-source geoscientific software.

### Running head
Polyhedral Gravity Modelling in Python

### Article Summary
> Please provide a 60-word summary (maximum) of your paper.

We present an open-source Python package for polyhedral full-tensor gravity forward modelling using Werner’s formulation with Numba acceleration. It computes gravitational potential, vector, and gradient tensor via speed- or memory-optimized modes, enabling scalable simulations from local to planetary scales. Validated against analytical and spherical harmonic solutions, it shows robustness, convergence, and high performance.
